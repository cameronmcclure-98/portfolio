Product Marketing
Product Brochures & Datasheets
Navius MRS-3 marine battery system, Rail Traction Battery Systems, Fortius-145 commercial vehicle battery
Published by Leclanché SA • Publicly available

Context

Leclanché sells battery systems into marine, rail and heavy-duty vehicle markets, where buyers are engineers and procurement teams. They need accurate specifications and a clear reason to choose a European-made system.

My role

Wrote all content and decided structure, image placement and style direction. Gathered and validated the technical data with Engineering and R&D. Design for Navius MRS-3 and Rail Traction was executed by an external agency from my brief; the Fortius-145 datasheet I designed and produced myself in Canva.

Approach

Each document pairs specification tables with plain-language value points, such as safety, cycle life, certification and European manufacturing, so the same asset works for technical evaluation and commercial conversations.

Outcome

Used by the sales team and at international trade fairs.
